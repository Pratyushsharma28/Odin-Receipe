PANEER BUTTER MASALA

DESCRIPTION

Paneer Butter Masala is a popular and delicious Indian dish made with soft paneer cubes cooked in a rich, creamy tomato-based gravy. It is prepared using butter, cream, and aromatic spices that give it a smooth texture and flavorful taste. It is best enjoyed with naan, roti, or rice.

INGRIEDENTS

  • 200g Paneer
  • 2 Tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons Butter
  • 1 Onion
  • Salt and Spices

STEPS

  1. Chop the tomatoes and onion
  2. Heat butter in a pan
  3. Cook onion,tomatoes and spices
  4. Add paneer and mix well.
  5. Cood for 5 minutes and serve
