Chole Recipe

Description

Chole is a popular North Indian dish made with chickpeas cooked in a spicy and flavorful gravy. It is prepared using onions, tomatoes, and aromatic Indian spices. Chole is best enjoyed with bhature, puri, or rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dried chickpeas (chole)
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 2 teaspoons chole masala
  • Salt as per taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves

Steps

  1. Soak the chickpeas overnight in water.
  2. Boil the chickpeas until soft.
  3. Heat oil in a pan.
  4. Add chopped onions and cook until golden.
  5. Add ginger-garlic paste and tomatoes.
  6. Add turmeric, chilli powder, and chole masala.
  7. Add boiled chickpeas and mix well.
  8. Add some water and cook for 10-15 minutes.
  9. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves.
  10. Serve hot with bhature or rice.
