Masala Dosa Recipe

Description

Masala Dosa is a popular South Indian dish made from fermented rice and lentil batter. It is crispy and filled with a delicious potato masala. Dosa is usually served with coconut chutney and sambar.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice
  • 1/2 cup urad dal
  • 3 boiled potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • Salt as per taste
  • Curry leaves

Steps

  1. Wash and soak rice and urad dal for 4-6 hours.
  2. Grind them into a smooth batter.
  3. Allow the batter to ferment overnight.
  4. Boil and mash the potatoes.
  5. Heat oil in a pan and add mustard seeds.
  6. Add onions, green chillies, and curry leaves.
  7. Add turmeric powder and mashed potatoes.
  8. Mix well and cook for 5 minutes.
  9. Heat a dosa pan and spread the batter thinly.
  10. Cook until the dosa becomes golden and crispy.
  11. Add the potato filling and fold the dosa.
  12. Serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar.
